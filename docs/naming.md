# Name: brush.ingsparks.de – Zahnputz-Timer für Kinder

Decided 2026-11-14.

## Brand rule for all ingsparks apps

The app's name is its domain written out (like del.icio.us back then), followed by an en dash
and a short German claim. Sibling apps: `check.ingsparks.de` (doctor appointments) and
`vacation.ingsparks.de – Familienurlaube planen und koordinieren`.

- Use `brush.ingsparks.de – Zahnputz-Timer für Kinder` as the wordmark, the tab title
  (`<title>`), the README heading and the web app manifest `name`.
- Short name on the home screen: `Zähneputzen`.
- The spelled-out "Brushing Sparks" is only how the domain reads aloud. It is not the brand and
  does not appear in the UI, the tab title or the README.

## Why `brush`

The domain mechanic of the ingsparks family stays: subdomain + `ingsparks` reads as a real phrase
that names the job, here "Brushing Sparks". Brushing is the action itself, not a category (not
"dental", "health" or "kids"). Quiet second meaning: a brushed tooth sparkles, and each brushing
lights a small spark in the forest (an open flower in the morning, a lantern in the evening).

Considered and dropped: `gleam.` (names the result, not the action), `morn.` (only half the job),
`shin.` (reads as a body part), `clean.` (sounds like a cleaning service).

## Claim

`Zahnputz-Timer für Kinder`: says plainly what the app is, in German, and is easy to find. The
in-app subline stays "Zwei Minuten, vier Ecken."
