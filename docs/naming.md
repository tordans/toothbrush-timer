# Name: Brushing Sparks

Picked with the ingsparks naming brief (subdomain + `ingsparks` = a real English phrase that names
the job of the product). Status: proposal, 2026-11-05.

## Pick: Brushing Sparks

1. **Domain:** `brush.ingsparks.de`, read aloud "Brushing Sparks".
2. **Why it names the job:** the product does exactly one thing: it keeps a child brushing for
   two minutes, morning and evening, and shows the week of brushings on a path through a forest.
   "Brushing" is the action itself, not a category (not "dental", not "health", not "kids").
   The quiet second meaning fits the family myth: a brushed tooth sparkles, and the small light
   that appears on the forest path after each brushing is a spark.
3. **Why it avoids the rejected patterns:** it is an action the child does, not a process word
   for software (`Syncing`), not a generic planner (`Planning`), not a family-myth word that could
   be any product (`Connecting`), and the English lands for German speakers too ("brush" is
   common in German product names). No flight imagery, so no overlap with Flying Sparks.
4. **Hero:**
   - Wordmark: **Brushing Sparks** (one name, one weight).
   - Subline: **Two minutes, four corners.** In the German UI: _Zwei Minuten, vier Ecken._
   - Support line: _Brush top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right. Pause any time. Watch the
     week fill up._ German: _Oben links, oben rechts, unten links, unten rechts. Jederzeit
     anhalten. Die Woche füllt sich._
   - Tab title: `Brushing Sparks`. Meta description: subline + "A calm two-minute toothbrushing
     timer for children, without points or rewards."

## Plan B: Gleaming Sparks

1. **Domain:** `gleam.ingsparks.de`, read aloud "Gleaming Sparks".
2. **Why:** names the result (clean, shiny teeth) and fits the spark myth well.
3. **Why second:** it names the outcome, not the action. A parent looking for "a toothbrushing
   timer" understands "Brushing" at once; "Gleaming" needs one beat of explanation.

## Rejected

| Candidate        | Domain               | Verdict                                                   |
| ---------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Morning Sparks   | `morn.ingsparks.de`  | Only half the job; evening brushing is just as important. |
| Shining Sparks   | `shin.ingsparks.de`  | "shin" reads as a body part in the URL.                   |
| Timing Sparks    | `tim.ingsparks.de`   | Taken (time zone tool).                                   |
| Cleaning Sparks  | `clean.ingsparks.de` | Sounds like a household cleaning service.                 |
| Twinkling Sparks | `twinkl.…`           | Domain stem does not work; too poetic for a tool.         |
